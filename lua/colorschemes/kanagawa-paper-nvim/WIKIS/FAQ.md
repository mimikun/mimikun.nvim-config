### Why a separate package instead of merging into the original Kanagawa?

The original Kanagawa aims to be [WCAG](https://github.com/rebelot/kanagawa.nvim#accessibility) compliant. Meeting the minimum contrast ratio for compliance is very difficult for this theme, especially given one of the primary goals is to _reduce_ contrast and achieve a more muted aesthetic.

I also had some ideas for improving the overall structure of the project, adding additional features etc. With all of that, the repos just ended up becoming too different for PRs to make sense.
