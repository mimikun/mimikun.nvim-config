## About

This wiki page lists some ports of this color scheme for other apps.

This page can be edited by anyone. If you ported this color scheme to another app, feel free to share it here ;)

Color reference: see this [post](https://www.sainnhe.dev/post/contributing-guide/#color-palette)

**Note:** The following projects are sorted lexicographically.

### IDE

- [Visual Studio](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=sainnhe.sonokai-vs) by [@sainnhe](https://github.com/sainnhe)
- [XCode](https://git.sainnhe.dev/sainnhe/sonokai-xcode) by [@sainnhe](https://github.com/sainnhe)
